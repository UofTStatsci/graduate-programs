Before You Apply

Your next
step.

  1. Check program requirements through the School of Graduate Studies.
  2. Begin your application online via the SGS portal.
  3. Prepare transcripts, references, CV, statement of purpose and any applicable tests.
  4. Submit by the posted deadline.

Graduate tuition and fees vary by program and student status. Consult the SGS Fee Schedule for current information.
